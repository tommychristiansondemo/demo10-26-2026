# SOP-002 Safety Reporting

SYNTHETIC policy document for the TrialScout teaching demo.

## Purpose

Sets timelines for reporting adverse events to the sponsor safety desk.

## Serious Adverse Events

Rule SR-1: investigators must report every serious adverse event (SAE) to the sponsor within 24 hours of awareness. Protocols must not specify a longer window.

## SUSARs

Fatal or life-threatening SUSARs are expedited to regulators within 7 calendar days; all other SUSARs within 15.
