# SOP-005 Clinical Data Management

SYNTHETIC policy document for the TrialScout teaching demo.

## Purpose

Sets timeliness expectations for electronic case report form (eCRF) data.

## Data Entry

Rule DM-1: visit data must be entered in the eCRF within 5 days of the visit.

## Query Resolution

Rule DM-2: data queries must be resolved within 10 business days; protocols may not relax this.
