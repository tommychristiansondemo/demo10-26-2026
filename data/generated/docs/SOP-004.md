# SOP-004 Eligibility and Screening

SYNTHETIC policy document for the TrialScout teaching demo.

## Purpose

Controls the screening period and escalation of high screen-failure rates.

## Screening Period

Rule ES-1: the screening period must not exceed 28 days from consent to randomization.

## Screen Failure Escalation

Rule ES-2: a site whose screen-failure rate exceeds 40% after 10 or more screened participants triggers an eligibility-criteria review with the medical monitor.
