# SOP-003 Visit Windows and Scheduling

SYNTHETIC policy document for the TrialScout teaching demo.

## Purpose

Limits how far a protocol visit may drift from its nominal day.

## Window Limits

Rule VW-1: Phase 1 protocols may allow at most +/-3 days. Rule VW-2: Phase 2 and Phase 3 protocols may allow at most +/-7 days for treatment-period visits.

## Out-of-window Visits

An out-of-window visit is a protocol deviation and must be logged within 5 business days.
