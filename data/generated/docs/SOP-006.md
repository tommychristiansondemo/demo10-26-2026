# SOP-006 Site Selection and Performance Management

SYNTHETIC policy document for the TrialScout teaching demo.

## Purpose

Defines objective site performance thresholds and remediation.

## Enrollment Performance

Rule SP-1: a site enrolling fewer than 0.5 participants per month after its first 3 active months enters a remediation plan.

## Quality Thresholds

Rule SP-2: more than 5 protocol deviations per 10 enrolled participants, or average query age above 15 days, triggers a for-cause monitoring visit.
