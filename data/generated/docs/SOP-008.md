# SOP-008 Data Privacy and AI Tool Use

SYNTHETIC policy document for the TrialScout teaching demo.

## Purpose

Protects participant privacy when using analytics and AI assistants.

## Identifiers

Participant names, contact details, medical record numbers and dates of birth must never be entered into AI assistants. Use coded participant IDs only.

## AI Assistant Use

AI assistants may summarize aggregate operational data and policies. They must not provide medical advice, dosing recommendations for individual patients, or unblind treatment assignments.
